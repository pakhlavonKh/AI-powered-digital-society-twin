## 2. Methodological & Forensic Audit Notes

1. **Forensic Catch — ÖSYM `2021` Tablo 11 Typesetting Erratum (`2021-EVAL:TABLO11-SOZ-4X`):** On page 53 of `yksdegrapor24122021.pdf`, the `SÖZ — Dört Kez Katılan` row prints `Toplam = 43.914`, which omits the `100–149` bin (`1,648`). The true bin sum (`45,562`) reconciles 100% with ÖSYM's own printed row percentages (`3,578 / 45,562 = 7.85%`, `13,891 / 45,562 = 30.49%`), Tablo 10 (`938,291`), and Tablo 12 (`938,291`). Both `source_printed_total` (`43914`) and `group_total_reconciled` (`45562`) are stored explicitly, with `verification_status='conflicting'` and an `erratum_note`.
2. **YÖK `T103` vs. `T101` Campus Granularity for SBÜ (`2025-2026:YOK-T103-SBU`):** In `2026_T103.xls`, YÖK aggregates *Sağlık Bilimleri Üniversitesi* faculties across Istanbul and Ankara without campus tags. Therefore `yok_ankara_department_gender_2025_2026.csv` strictly covers the **21 Ankara-headquartered universities** (with `campus_city_inferred='KKTC'` separating KKTC branch units), while SBÜ's Gülhane Ankara campus (`768` Önlisans + `1,089` Lisans = `1,857` new registrants in `2025–2026`) is cleanly isolated via the `KEÇİÖREN` sub-row in `yok_university_new_entrants_2023_2026.csv`.
3. **Pre-2022 Structural Break Flag:** All tables derived from `2021` ÖSYM Evaluation and YÖK Mobility reports carry `pre_2022_baraj_regime_flag = 1` or `exam_year = 2021` and must be used strictly as **structural priors** re-weighted via IPF to `2022–2026` marginals (`osym_national_applicants_2019_2026.csv`, `osym_cumulative_score_distributions_2019_2026.csv`, `yok_entrant_age_gender_2025_2026.csv`, and `meb_province_12th_grade_cohorts_2025_2026.csv`).
